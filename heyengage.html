The Problem

Most organisations want to engage well.
Few know how.

Community engagement is one of the most critical capabilities an organisation can have — and one of the most underdeveloped. Most teams learn on the job, repeat the same mistakes, and wonder why communities don't trust them.

HeyEngage exists to change that. It's a practical, evidence-based education platform built on Becky's 27 years of frontline experience. Every course, program and resource is designed to build genuine, durable capability — not just compliance.
