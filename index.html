The Journey

27 Years of Impact

1997

The Beginning

Becky begins her career in community development in the United Kingdom, working directly with marginalised communities in public consultation processes.

2005

Australia Calls

Relocates to Australia, bringing a fresh perspective to community engagement across government and not-for-profit sectors. Begins building a national reputation.

2012

Author & Thought Leader

Publishes her first major work on participatory practice. Takes to the conference stage as a keynote speaker, challenging orthodoxies in the sector.

2018

Board Director & Advocate

Joins the boards of leading industry organisations. Uses governance influence to reshape how community engagement is valued and practiced at sector level.

2021

HeyEngage Launches

Launches HeyEngage — an education ecosystem dedicated to building community engagement capability in organisations across Australia and beyond.

Now

A Global Movement

A podcast, a global community, ongoing advocacy, and consulting work that touches hundreds of organisations. The mission: unchanged. The scale: growing.
